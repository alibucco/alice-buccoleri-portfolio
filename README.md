# Alice Buccoleri

### Computer Graphics · AI · Creative Technology

I am a multidisciplinary engineer working where **technology, visual storytelling and interaction design** meet. My work moves between computer graphics, AI-powered systems, 3D environments and audiovisual production.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in **Cinema and Media Engineering** and I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in **Computer Engineering**, specialising in **Computer Graphics and Multimedia**. This path gives me a hybrid perspective: I combine the technical rigour of software and computing with an interest in images, narrative and creative experiences.

> From a cinematic story to an interactive system, I enjoy turning ideas into experiences people can see, use and remember.
