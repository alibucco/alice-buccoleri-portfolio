# Transmedia Projects

## Overview
This repository presents two related academic projects developed in the field of transmedia storytelling, interaction design and artificial intelligence ethics.

The work combines narrative design, critical thinking on AI systems, and collaboration with external organizations.

---

## Transmedia ARG Project

[ARG Walkthrough](https://youtu.be/mJMIF9Sod8w)

An Alternate Reality Game (ARG) developed in collaboration with the Design Department (DAD) at Politecnico di Torino.

### Concept
The project explores a fictional design company entirely driven by automation, where creativity is suppressed.

The narrative follows a worker who challenges the system by breaking ethical and organizational rules, ultimately transforming the company's principles and removing its leadership.

### Goals
- Explore the relationship between automation and creativity  
- Develop a transmedia storytelling experience  
- Design narrative-driven interaction across multiple media

## Hackathon – AI Ethics Interactive Experience

[Website](https://waluigi.rizosfera.org/)

Project developed during a hackathon in collaboration with dmstfctn and Rizosfera.

### Concept
An interactive game that reverses the traditional AI training paradigm:  
the AI acts as the trainer, while the human becomes the subject being trained.

Through progressive decision-making scenarios, the experience challenges the idea of AI objectivity.

### Key Themes
- AI ethics and responsibility  
- Bias and human influence in training systems  
- Conflict between algorithmic decisions and human values
