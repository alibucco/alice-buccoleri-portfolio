# Transmedia Storytelling & Interactive Design

Two academic projects that combine narrative design, interaction and a critical perspective on technology.

## Transmedia ARG

### Alternate Reality Game · Design Department, Politecnico di Torino

📺 **[Watch the walkthrough](https://youtu.be/mJMIF9Sod8w)**

The walkthrough is the best way to experience the project: it shows how the story unfolds across the different media, channels and interactions designed for the game.

The ARG is set in a fictional design company run entirely by automation, where creativity has been removed from the workplace. The player follows a worker who challenges the system, breaks its ethical and organisational rules, and ultimately helps transform the company's principles.

**Focus:** Transmedia storytelling · Narrative design · Interactive experience · Automation and creativity

## AI Ethics Interactive Experience

### Hackathon project · Team AIncredible

🌐 **[Visit the interactive experience](https://waluigi.rizosfera.org/)**

Developed during a hackathon in collaboration with dmstfctn and Rizosfera, this interactive game reverses the conventional AI-training paradigm: the AI acts as the trainer, while the human becomes the subject being trained.

Through progressive decision-making scenarios, the experience questions the idea of AI objectivity and examines how human choices, bias and responsibility shape algorithmic systems.

**Team:** AIncredible

**Focus:** Interaction design · AI ethics · Bias · Responsible technology
