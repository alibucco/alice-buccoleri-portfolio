# Overview: Myosotis

Myosotis is a poignant short film that explores the intersection of memory, illness, and the enduring power of art. 

The project centers on the daily life of Adele, an elderly woman living with Alzheimer’s disease, and the emotional bridge that music provides between her fragmented present and her vibrant past. 

## Inspiration & Personal Roots
While the project was born from the team's personal experiences with loved ones, it is directly inspired by the viral story of Marta C. González, a former prima ballerina whose muscle memory was reawakened by the notes of Swan Lake. This film aims to reinterpret that "harsh and concrete" reality with delicacy, transforming a medical condition into a soul-stirring moment of liberation.

## Synopsis
The story follows an elderly lady whose condition prevents her from recognizing her own daughter or performing basic daily tasks. 
She lives in a state of quiet confusion until she hears the notes of Ludwig van Beethoven's "Moonlight Sonata". This melody, which accompanied her during her youth on prestigious stages, triggers a "liberating gesture"—a momentary lapse in the darkness of her illness where her body remembers the grace and movements of her time as a professional dancer.  

## Artistic Significance
Symbolism: The title Myosotis refers to the scientific name of the "Forget-Me-Not" flower. 
It serves as a fil rouge (common thread) throughout the film, appearing in the young dancer’s hair and later as a pendant worn by the elderly Adele, symbolizing a love and memory that remains "uncorruptible".  

The Body Remembers: The film’s tagline, "The body remembers what the mind forgets," encapsulates the theme that physical memory and emotional resonance can persist even when cognitive functions fail.  

## Production & Technical Details
Format: 2K Flat, Audio 2.0 (5:18 min).

Cinematography: Shot with a 35mm lens for theatrical sequences to emphasize choreography, and 50mm/85mm lenses for intimate, domestic close-ups.

Lighting: Natural light was augmented with warm LED panels and gels to create a comfortable, neutral atmosphere.
