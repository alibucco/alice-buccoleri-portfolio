# Myosotis

### Short film · 5 min 18 sec · 2K Flat · Audio 2.0

**Myosotis** is a short film about memory, Alzheimer's disease and the power of art to create a bridge between a fragmented present and a vivid past.

🎬 **[Watch the short film on YouTube](https://youtu.be/JEi3JJYDkQ4)**

## Logline

Unable to recognise her daughter or manage everyday gestures, an elderly former dancer seems lost in her own world. When a familiar piano melody begins to play, her body remembers the movements that her mind can no longer hold.

## Story

Adele lives in quiet confusion, separated from the memories that once defined her life. Beethoven's *Moonlight Sonata* becomes an emotional trigger: for a moment, the music reconnects her to the stage and to the dancer she used to be. Through movement, the film explores the possibility that physical and emotional memory can persist even when language and recognition fade.

The title refers to the scientific name of the **forget-me-not** flower. It appears as a visual thread between Adele's past and present, symbolising a bond with memory that remains intact.

> **The body remembers what the mind forgets.**

## Inspiration

The screenplay was inspired by the story of **Marta C. González**, a former ballerina living with Alzheimer's disease. In a widely shared video published by the Spanish association Música para Despertar, she responds to Tchaikovsky's *Swan Lake* by recalling gestures from the choreography she had performed decades earlier. *Myosotis* is an original fictional work that reinterprets this idea through Adele's story and a different musical motif.

## My contribution

**Production** — I coordinated the practical and organisational work that made the project possible despite having no production budget.

- **Casting:** I organised several casting sessions with actors made available through Torino Film Commission.
- **Casting the dancer:** I found the dancer through CUS Torino, identifying the performer for the role at the centre of the film's visual language.
- **Locations:** I researched and secured the filming locations.
- **Coordination:** I brought together the people, places and logistical elements needed to turn the creative vision into a shoot.

## Visual approach

- **Cinematography:** a 35 mm lens for the theatrical sequences, paired with 50 mm and 85 mm lenses for close, domestic moments.
- **Lighting:** natural light supplemented with warm LED panels and gels, creating an intimate and restrained atmosphere.
- **Visual motif:** the forget-me-not flower links the young dancer's hair to the pendant worn by the older Adele.
